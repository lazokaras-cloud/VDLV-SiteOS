# Glossary and language register

You will not know most of this. Do not guess from context: several terms mean
something specific and expensive.

## Contract and commercial

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **JBCC** | Joint Building Contracts Committee. The standard form of contract in South African building. Several editions in use at once. |
| **PBA** | Principal Building Agreement. The main JBCC contract between employer and contractor. |
| **PA** | Principal Agent. The employer's representative who issues instructions and certificates. Contractual deadlines run to and from him. |
| **PQS** | Professional Quantity Surveyor. Prepares the bills, values the work, certifies payment. Independent of the contractor. |
| **BoQ** | Bill of Quantities. The priced schedule of everything to be built, in trade bills. |
| **P&G** | Preliminaries and General. The cost of *running* the job rather than building it: staff, offices, plant, insurance. Split time / value / fixed. |
| **SI** | Site Instruction. A written instruction from the PA. The event that starts a variation clock. |
| **VO** | Variation Order. A change to the contract works, priced at actual cost plus markup. |
| **EOT** | Extension of Time. A claim for more time, which carries time-related P&G with it. |
| **PC** | Practical Completion. The works are usable. Starts several other clocks. |
| **Works completion** | After PC. Starts the defects liability period. |
| **Final completion** | After defects are made good. Starts latent defects liability. |
| **Contra charge** | A cost the contractor recovers from a sub-contractor by deducting it. Must be deducted before the certificate. |
| **Retention** | A percentage held back from each certificate, released at completion. |
| **Provisional sum** | A budgetary allowance by the PQS for specialist work, to be executed by a nominated or selected sub-contractor. Can be most of a tender. |
| **Prime cost (PC) amount** | Like a provisional sum, for supplied articles. Net, including delivery. |
| **Nominated sub-contractor** | Chosen by the employer or PA. Treated as lower risk to the contractor. |
| **Selected sub-contractor** | Chosen from a list, contractor carries more risk. |
| **Attendance** | Services the contractor gives a specialist free of charge: access, scaffold, power, offloading. A priced bill line. |
| **Special attendance** | Beyond general attendance. Usually excluded by qualification. |
| **Deemed refused** | The PA failed to respond within his window, so the claim counts as refused and becomes a dispute. Useful, not a failure. |
| **Deemed complete** | The PA failed to inspect within his window, so the works count as complete. |
| **Tender qualification** | A stated exclusion or assumption attached to a tender. Unqualified risk is risk the contractor owns. |
| **Adjudication** | Comparing sub-contractor quotes to choose one. Not the legal dispute sense. |
| **Rate build-up** | Constructing a rate from material, labour and plant when nobody will quote. |
| **Cost report** | The accounting view of what the job has actually cost. Reconciled against the measure. |
| **Allowable** | What the bill says the work should cost. Allowable against actual is where saving shows. |
| **Cashflow** | Forecast of certificates over time, from the priced bill and the baseline programme. |
| **Baseline programme** | The programme at contract signature. Everything is measured against it. |

## South African specifics

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Builders' break** | Industry shutdown, roughly mid-December to mid-January. **Not a public holiday.** Carried in the contract via qualifications. |
| **BBBEE** | Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment. Contributor levels 1 to 8; level 2 is strong. |
| **SASRIA** | State insurer for special risks, riot and civil commotion. A separate policy. |
| **FEM** | Federated Employers Mutual. Workmen's compensation insurer for building. A "letter of good standing" comes from them. |
| **OHS Act** | Occupational Health and Safety Act, 1993. Drives the safety file and appointments. |
| **MBA / MBA North** | Master Builders Association. Publishes the builders' break dates. |
| **Erf** | A land parcel or stand. Also used on site to mean the site itself. |
| **Snag** | A defect on the completion list. |
| **Bakkie** | Pickup truck. Where the site agent does his admin. |

## Systems

| Term | Meaning |

| **Candy** | CCS estimating and programming package. Produces bills and programmes. Entrenched. |
| **BuildSmart** | RIB accounting package. Cost reports, creditors, remittances. Entrenched. |

## Afrikaans register

This is the part most likely to be got wrong. The product's Afrikaans is **how a
Gauteng building site actually talks**, not textbook Afrikaans.

**The rule:** Afrikaans sentence structure and everyday vocabulary; technical
nouns stay English, because that is what is said out loud. Use contractions.

### Terms that stay English

notice, claim, contra, markup, VO, PA, SI, PQS, BoQ, P&G, plant, snag, rate,
certificate, retention, default, adjudication, settlement discount, lead time,
critical path, baseline, cut-off, practical completion, final account, edition.

### Terms that become Afrikaans

| English | Afrikaans |
| --- | --- |
| site (the place) | erf |
| working day | werksdag |
| drawing | tekening |
| supplier | verskaffer |
| week / month | week / maand |
| notice period remaining | tyd oor |
| on time | betyds |
| behind | agter |
| rework | oordoen |
| approved | goedgekeur |
| contract value | kontrakwaarde |
| margin | margin *(stays English by preference)* |
| contingency | res waarde |

### Worked examples

Textbook, wrong register:

> Kennisgewing van die uitgawe moet die hoofagent binne 20 werksdae bereik nadat
> u daarvan bewus geword het.

Site register, right:

> Die notice moet binne 20 werksdae by die PA wees vandat jy van die koste weet.
> Mis jy dit, is die claim uit tyd uit. Maak nie saak hoe goed dit is nie.

More:

- "Rots in die lift pit, geen VO opgestel nie"
- "Default notice loop uit oor 1 werksdag"
- "Eerste ding: het julle al daar begin werk?"
- "Dan's daar nog 'n claim ook."
- "Vier minute se werk, R303 440 op die certificate."

### Voice

Spoken numbers must parse: *"vier en sestig duisend twee honderd"* → `64200`.
Afrikaans places units before tens, so the parser cannot assume English order.

### Document references

Identifiers, never translated. `SI-114` stays `SI-114`. An instruction number was
issued by the principal agent and translating it makes it a different document.

### When you cannot write it

Ship the English and mark the key `// TODO af`. Do not machine-translate contract
terms: a wrong word in a notice is a contractual problem, not a typo.
